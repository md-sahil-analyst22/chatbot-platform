# Appointments, fees and policies

> Demo content. ClinicCare is a fictional clinic created for a product demo. All prices are
> examples.

## Booking an appointment

- Call the front desk or book through this chat.
- Walk-ins are welcome; booked patients are seen first.
- Please arrive 10 minutes early for your first visit to fill in a registration form.
- Bring previous prescriptions and reports if you have them.

## Consultation fees

| Doctor | First visit | Follow-up within 7 days |
|---|---|---|
| General physician | ₹500 | Free |
| Paediatrician | ₹600 | Free |

Fees are paid at the front desk by UPI, card or cash.

## Cancellations

- You can cancel or reschedule an appointment free of charge up to 2 hours before the time.
- Report and package payments are refunded in full if the sample has not been collected yet.

## Privacy

- Your reports are shared only with you and the doctor you consult.
- We never share your phone number or reports with anyone for marketing.
