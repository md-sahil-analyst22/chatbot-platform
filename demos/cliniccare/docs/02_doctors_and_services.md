## Paediatrics

- Dr. P. Ghosh (MBBS, DCH): Monday, Wednesday and Friday, 10:00 AM - 2:00 PM.
- Child check-ups, growth monitoring and advice for common childhood illnesses.

## Diagnostic sample collection

- Blood and urine samples are collected from 8:00 AM to 11:00 AM every day the clinic is open.
- Fasting tests (for example fasting sugar and lipid profile) need 10-12 hours of fasting; water is
  allowed.
- Most reports are ready within 24 hours and are sent on WhatsApp as a PDF.
- Samples are processed by a partner NABL-accredited laboratory.
