# About ClinicCare Family Clinic

> Demo content. ClinicCare is a fictional clinic created for a product demo.

ClinicCare is a family clinic in Behala, Kolkata offering general medicine, paediatrics,
gynaecology and diagnostic sample collection.

## Timings
- Monday to Saturday: 8:00 AM - 9:00 PM
- Sunday: 9:00 AM - 1:00 PM
- Closed on Durga Puja Dashami, Diwali and Republic Day
