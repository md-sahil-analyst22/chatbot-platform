# ITR documents checklist

> Demo content. TaxMitra Associates is a fictional firm created for a product demo.

Please send these documents before your consultation, by email or through the upload link we share.
Clear phone photos are fine; passwords for PDFs should be sent separately.

## Everyone

- PAN card and Aadhaar card (Aadhaar must be linked to PAN).
- Bank account details for the refund (account number and IFSC).
- Login for the income tax portal, or we will help you reset it.
- Form 26AS and AIS (we can download them with your portal login).

## Salaried

- Form 16 from every employer for the year.
- Salary slips for March, if Form 16 is not ready yet.
- Rent receipts and the landlord's PAN (if annual rent is above ₹1 lakh) for HRA.

## Investments and deductions

- Section 80C proofs: PPF, ELSS, life insurance premium, tuition fees, home loan principal.
- Section 80D: health insurance premium receipts for self, family and parents.
- Home loan interest certificate from the bank.
- Donation receipts with the trust's PAN for section 80G.

## Capital gains

- Capital gains statement from your broker (Zerodha, Groww, Upstox etc.).
- Mutual fund capital gains statement from CAMS and KFintech.
- Sale deed and purchase deed if you sold a house or land.

## Deadlines

- 31 July: last date for most individual returns without audit.
- 31 December: last date for a belated or revised return.
- We need your documents at least **5 working days** before a deadline to guarantee filing on time.
