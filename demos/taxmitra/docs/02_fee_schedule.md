# TaxMitra Associates: fee schedule (individuals)

> Demo content. TaxMitra Associates is a fictional firm created for a product demo. All prices are
> examples.

All fees are in Indian rupees and **exclude 18% GST**. Fees are payable before filing. Prices are
valid for assessment year 2026-27.

## Income tax return (ITR) filing

| Service | Who it is for | Fee |
|---|---|---|
| ITR-1 basic | Salary from one employer, interest income, no capital gains | ₹999 |
| ITR-2 standard | Salary plus capital gains from shares or mutual funds | ₹2,499 |
| ITR-2 with foreign assets | Residents holding foreign shares or bank accounts | ₹4,999 |
| ITR-3 professional | Doctors, consultants, freelancers with books of account | ₹5,999 |
| ITR-4 presumptive | Small businesses and professionals under section 44AD/44ADA | ₹2,999 |

## Add-ons

- Revised return (our own filing, within the same year): free.
- Revised return for a return filed by someone else: ₹1,499.
- Belated return (filed after 31 July): ₹500 extra.
- Response to a simple income tax notice (section 143(1) mismatch): ₹1,999.
- Capital gains computation for more than 50 trades: ₹1,000 per additional 100 trades.

## Discounts

- Returning clients get 10% off ITR filing fees.
- Families filing three or more returns together get 15% off each return.
- Students and first-time filers with income below ₹5 lakh pay ₹499 for ITR-1.

## Payment

We accept UPI, net banking and debit/credit cards. Cash is accepted only at the office. An invoice
with GST is emailed after payment.

## Refunds

If we have not started work, the full fee is refunded within 7 working days. Once a return has been
prepared and shared with you for approval, the fee is not refundable.
