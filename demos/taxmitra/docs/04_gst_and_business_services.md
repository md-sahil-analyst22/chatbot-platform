# GST and business services

> Demo content. TaxMitra Associates is a fictional firm created for a product demo. All prices are
> examples and exclude 18% GST.

## Monthly GST return filing

| Business size (annual turnover) | Returns included | Monthly fee |
|---|---|---|
| Up to ₹40 lakh | GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B | ₹1,499 |
| ₹40 lakh to ₹2 crore | GSTR-1, GSTR-3B and input tax credit reconciliation | ₹2,999 |
| Above ₹2 crore | Everything above plus monthly review call | ₹5,999 |

Quarterly filers under the QRMP scheme pay the same fee per quarter.

GSTR-9 annual return is charged separately at one month's fee.

## Bookkeeping

- Monthly bookkeeping in Tally or Zoho Books: from ₹3,000 per month for up to 100 transactions.
- Each additional 100 transactions: ₹1,000.

## Startups

- Company incorporation (private limited) with two directors: ₹14,999 including government fees
  for authorised capital up to ₹1 lakh.
- Startup India (DPIIT) recognition application: ₹4,999.

## How we work with businesses

1. A free 15-minute call to understand your business.
2. You share sales and purchase data by the 5th of every month.
3. We file GSTR-1 by the 11th and GSTR-3B by the 20th.
4. You receive a filing confirmation on WhatsApp and email.

Late data from the client can lead to late fees from the government; those late fees are paid by the
client.
